# founders.click — launch readiness audit (2026-09-02)

Audited at `1227508` → fixes landed as `2ce7593` on `main`. Evidence-based:
every "verified" below names the command, run, or browser session that
produced it; everything else is labelled as what it is.

**Recommendation: NO-SHIP for paying customers. NOT READY for open invites.
READY for a closed, unpaid design-partner cohort once the two P0 evidence
gaps below are closed — which is a matter of hours, not weeks.**

## 1. Executive summary

founders.click is a multi-tenant growth engine for Sharetribe marketplace
operators: it syncs marketplace listings, generates and publishes SEO landing
pages, serves them on the customer's own domain through a Cloudflare edge
proxy, and bills by published-page capacity through Stripe. The customer is a
marketplace operator; the end user of the output is a search engine and the
operator's future buyer.

What is genuinely solid: the deployment pipeline proves which commit is
serving; the entitlement gate is atomic and write-locked at the privilege
level; the edge proxy fails open for customer traffic and degrades gracefully;
tenant isolation is consistently enforced server-side; the legal pages are
real; the marketing site, help centre, signup, login, password reset and 404
behave correctly in a browser, on desktop and (after one fix) on mobile.

What blocks shipping is not one bug but two unclosed questions and one
unverified core path:

1. **Transactional email has never been seen arriving.** Two real emails were
   sent to the founder's inbox during this audit (a signup confirmation at
   08:38 UTC on 2026-09-01 and a password reset ~03:20 UTC on 2026-09-02).
   Neither has been confirmed received; the account was still unconfirmed 19
   hours later. If EmailIt is accepting sends but not delivering, every
   signup dead-ends at "check your email".
2. **No page has ever been published through the production pipeline.** The
   golden path — create → publish → serve on `/a/` → sitemap → canonical →
   JSON-LD — has unit coverage and a post-publish contract validator, but the
   live smoke still `SKIP`s its tenant-page check because there is nothing to
   check. The authenticated per-feature browser audit was blocked by the same
   unconfirmed account; it is armed and fires automatically when the account
   confirms.
3. **Billing is not safe to charge on.** The Stripe → database projection is
   untested end to end, trials never expire, a missed webhook is permanent and
   undetectable, refunds and disputes are unhandled, and the product still
   sells "AI credits" as a customer-facing SKU in three places despite the
   documented decision not to.

The audit also fixed, tested and deployed the defects that needed no product
decision (§5).

## 5. Fixed in this audit (`2ce7593`)

All contained, no product decision, each with tests:

- Security headers on every response (`src/lib/security-headers.ts`, 22 assertions): nosniff, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy everywhere; `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and HSTS on platform routes/hosts only — tenant pages on customer hostnames deliberately excluded.
- Stored-XSS path closed: JSON-LD serialised through `safeJsonLd()` (escapes `<`, U+2028/9) on tenant pages, help articles, landing (`src/lib/json-ld.ts`).
- Open redirect closed: login `?next=` restricted to same-origin paths (`src/lib/safe-next.ts`).
- Rate limiters read `cf-connecting-ip` before the client-appendable `X-Forwarded-For` (`src/lib/public-rate-limit.ts`, shared by four routes).
- `submitSupportTicket` (two emails per call, one to a caller-chosen address) limited to 5/min/IP; help feedback and search limited; `sitemap-by-host` validated and limited.
- `createConversation` asserts workspace membership like its siblings.
- Auth pages `noindex`; signup states Terms/Privacy acceptance with links.
- Mobile header overflow (LanguageSwitcher ignored `hidden`).
- Four real lint errors.
- `tests/e2e/public-journeys.py` and `tests/e2e/feature-audit.py` added as repeatable evidence generators.

## 10. Answers

1. **Can we safely invite customers today?** No. Not until an inbox proves
   email delivery and one page has been published and served on production.
   Both are hours of work and the tooling to prove them is in the repo.
2. **Can we safely charge customers today?** No. The webhook projection is
   untested, trials never end, a missed webhook is permanent, refunds are
   unhandled, and the customer-facing SKU set contradicts the billing model.
3. **Five highest-value actions.** (1) Confirm the account, run the
   feature audit and the golden path. (2) Prove/fix EmailIt delivery.
   (3) Error reporting + alert route + cron liveness, and set `CRON_SECRET`.
   (4) Billing hardening set + run the credentialed billing tests.
   (5) Capture the base schema and confirm backups.
4. **Decisions required from the founder.** Trial expiry behaviour and
   downgrade policy (pricing); remove the credits SKU or keep it; launch
   with or without custom domains; account-deletion approach; whether to
   keep the Lovable AI gateway; set `CRON_SECRET`; delete `.lovable/`,
   stale Cloudflare tokens, the `CLOUD_WORKER` secret; apex cutover and
   workers.dev disable; the six-locale switcher.
